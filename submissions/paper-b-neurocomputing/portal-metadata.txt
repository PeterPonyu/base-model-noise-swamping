TITLE
========================================
Knowledge-Edit Effects Under Real INT8 and NF4 Quantization: Efficacy Survival and Damage-Rank Stability

========================================
ABSTRACT (replace verbatim from final compiled PDF after review; current draft)
========================================
We measure how knowledge-edit effects behave under real post-training INT8 and NF4 quantization. Across three models (Llama-3.2-1B L12, Llama-3.2-3B L24, Qwen-2.5-1.5B L21), three editors (ROME, MEMIT, AlphaEdit), three seeds, and 27 model-editor-seed cells, we apply the bitsandbytes INT8 vectorwise and NF4 double-quant codec kernels to the edited MLP and to the full model. ROME efficacy exceeds the preregistered 0.80 NF4 threshold on the primary Llama-3.2-1B and Qwen-2.5-1.5B cells. The stronger prediction that MEMIT and AlphaEdit are no less robust than ROME holds on every Llama-1B arm but fails on the Qwen NF4 edited-layer and full-model arms; AlphaEdit also trails ROME marginally under Qwen INT8 full-model. Qwen absolute edit-success rates in FP32 are 0.452 (ROME), 0.468 (MEMIT), 0.452 (AlphaEdit); under NF4 full-model they fall to 0.405, 0.410, 0.385 respectively, and the conditional survival given FP32 succeeded is 0.852 (ROME), 0.832 (MEMIT), 0.811 (AlphaEdit). Damage-rank survival is preserved under INT8 and edited-layer NF4 on both validated-law ROME cells. Under full-model NF4, the legacy flattened-grid rank passes at Llama-3.2-1B L12 (0.904) but fails at Llama-3.2-3B L24 (0.680); the v1.2.1 repair artefact distinguishes this from the operational edit-level signed-mean rank, which remains near-unity on both validated-law ROME cells (Llama-1B 0.997 [0.996,0.998]; Llama-3B 0.997 [0.988,0.998]). The amended channel-scale x NF4 minimum-gap K3 axis remains UNADJUDICATED pending a targeted rerun; the legacy/local-gap sensitivity values that the readout reports are not used for directionality. INT8 has no outlier-decomposed bin-width axis (threshold = 0 in this paper's accounting), so its F_above and median-ratio columns are reported only for completeness and are not adjudicated against the amended K3 axis. A descriptive reconstruction-gap signature (r_func < r_param in every C3 scheme block; median r_func/r_param 0.00570 at INT8 and 0.00591 at NF4) is reported as a summary of the total-rounding error budget; the epsilon is implemented as Q(W+d)-(W+d), so the statistic is a gap, not a quantized-update error, and the 18/18 ordering is descriptive evidence, not a unique mechanism. Generation checks at n = 40 prompts show mean perplexity rises of 7.86-18.91% from FP32-edited to NF4 full-model across cells; paraphrase ESR is unchanged within noise at the edited-layer arms and mildly attenuated at full model. The preregistration document was a DRAFT (not ratified as active) and is included for the record; the core thresholds were specified prospectively in the design document, and the prereg carries dated protocol-level entries on the bin-width denominator and the F1 definition whose chronological status (pre- vs. post-data) is not adjudicated here, since the DRAFT remained unratified and the preregistration document's mtimes fall after several real cells. The Llama-3.1-8B cell from the prereg grid was omitted from this submission. The 40-edit generation check protocol was executed as part of Track 1 and its numbers are reported in Section 6; the deployed-kernel (Track 2, GPTQ/AWQ) bridge measurement is out of scope of this submission.
