Dear Editor-in-Chief,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "When and Why Does Key Geometry
Predict Locate-then-Edit Collateral Damage? A Closed-Form, Editor- and
Architecture-Conditioned Account of the Llama Family, with a Causal Test,"
for publication as a Full Paper in IEEE Transactions on Emerging Topics in
Computational Intelligence.

Knowledge editing of large language models is an emerging computational-
intelligence topic with a growing reliability problem: rank-one "locate-then-
edit" updates damage unrelated knowledge. The manuscript contributes a
closed-form mechanism for that damage, a confound-clean measurement protocol,
a causal null-space-projection test, and a six-model, four-family
characterization of where the mechanism holds, attenuates, or inverts —
including honest negative results delimiting its scope.

The manuscript is submitted solely to this journal, has not been published or
submitted elsewhere, and has not been posted to a preprint server. The review
version is fully anonymized per the journal's double-anonymous policy. I am
the sole author, and I declare no conflict of interest.
