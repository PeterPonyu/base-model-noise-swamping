\section{Deletion Collateral: Geometry Governs a New Edit Type}
\label{sec:deletion}

The mechanism was derived and tested for knowledge \emph{rewrites}. We show
it also governs \emph{deletion} edits --- removing a learned refusal
(``I can't help with that'') --- a qualitatively different edit direction
that was not in the original design. Using the same within-probe statistic
on Llama-3.2-1B L12 (Figure~\ref{fig:deletion}):

\subsection{Refusal-deletion collateral is geometry-governed}
It is governed \emph{more} strongly than rewrites: within-probe
(non-double-centered) $\rho$ \delRefNonDCa{} / \delRefNonDCb{} /
\delRefNonDCc{} (3 seeds; double-centered $\rho$ \delRefDCa{} /
\delRefDCb{} / \delRefDCc{}), above the rewrite reference \rewriteRef{}.
Layer profile (raw key-cosine within-probe, 3-seed): L8
\delRefLeight{}\,$\pm$\,\delRefLeightSD{} / L14
\delRefLfourteen{}\,$\pm$\,\delRefLfourteenSD{} (with the L12 raw aggregate
\delRefAgg{}; the gate \SxC{} arm agrees).

\subsection{Variant robustness: two distinct statistics, not to be conflated}
Two statistics exist per variant and must be reported separately: (a) the
\emph{gate \SxC{} statistic} (seed-0): the non-double-centered (non-DC)
\SxC{}-versus-damage Spearman and its double-centered (DC) value; (b) the
\emph{raw key-cosine within-probe statistic} (3-seed). These are different
quantities computed on different pipelines, and neither is a more-precise
or less-precise estimate of the other.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{eos} is robust on \emph{both}: gate non-DC \SxC{}
  \eosGateSxC{} (seed-0), and raw key-cosine within-probe
  \eosRaw{}\,$\pm$\,\eosRawSD{} (3-seed).
\item \emph{suppress} is the \emph{fragile} variant: its gate non-DC
  \SxC{} is \suppressGateSxC{} (seed-0) but double-centering-fragile
  ($\rightarrow\sim$\suppressGateDC{}); and on the raw key-cosine
  within-probe metric it is \suppressRaw{}\,$\pm$\,\suppressRawSD{}
  (3-seed, DEAD). These are \emph{different} statistics, not a discrepancy:
  the weak raw coupling is consistent with the double-centering fragility,
  so we report suppress as the fragile variant, never swapping the two
  numbers or averaging them into one.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Causal replica}
AlphaEdit-delete collapses damage \alphaDelDmgPre{}$\rightarrow\sim$\alphaDelDmgPost{}
\emph{and} the coupling to \alphaDelRho{}\,$\pm$\,\alphaDelRhoSD{} (3-seed,
DEAD) --- the same ``removes the geometry-carried damage whatever its
sign'' result reported for the rewrite arm in Section~\ref{sec:causal}.

\subsection{Llama-scoping replicates in deletion}
Qwen-1.5B-delete gives $\rho$ \qwenDelRho{}\,$\pm$\,\qwenDelRhoSD{} (3-seed)
at mean damage \qwenDelDmg{} (improvement regime) --- the same off-Llama
behavior documented for the rewrite arm in Section~\ref{sec:dissociation}.

\subsection{Transplant gate}
The \SxC{} surrogate beats a learned transplant baseline: $\Delta\rho$
(\SxC{} minus \emph{best} transplant) is \transDeltaLtwelveA{} /
\transDeltaLtwelveB{} / \transDeltaLtwelveC{} at L12 (seeds),
\transDeltaLeight{} at L8, and \transDeltaLfourteen{} at L14.

\subsection{Deletion transfers to an alternative fact dataset}
Refusal-deletion on the zsRE dataset \citep{levy2017zsre} at L10
(single-seed) gives: raw key-cosine within-probe \zsreDelRaw{} (PASS,
survives the permutation battery); formal gate \SxC{} non-DC \zsreDelNonDC{}
$\rightarrow$ DC \zsreDelDC{} (\emph{double-centering-robust}, the opposite
of suppress's fragility on CounterFact). This sits below the zsRE rewrite
law (\zsreRewrite{}, same layer, Section~\ref{sec:generality}) and below
CounterFact deletion (\delRefNonDCa{}, L12), so geometry governs deletion
collateral across datasets, attenuating with the weaker zsRE signal already
noted for the rewrite arm. All zsRE-deletion numbers in this section are
single-seed and should be read as a transfer check, not as a
seed-matched replication of the CounterFact deletion result above.
