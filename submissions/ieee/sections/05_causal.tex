\section{Causal Test: Null-Space Projection Removes Geometry-Predicted Damage}
\label{sec:causal}

The within-probe correlation (Section~\ref{sec:regime}) and the \SxC{}
algebra (Section~\ref{sec:method}) are, on their own, observational. We
close the gap with an intervention. AlphaEdit \citep{fang2025alphaedit}
applies the same rank-one target as ROME but first projects the update onto
the null-space of a set of preserved-knowledge keys, so for a preserved
probe $k_p$ the induced perturbation $\dW_\alpha\cdot k_p\approx 0$.
Eq.~\ref{eq:sc} then predicts: since ROME's collateral term is
$S\cdot\|k_p\|\cdot|C|$ and AlphaEdit drives it toward zero, the
\emph{damage AlphaEdit removes} should itself rise monotonically with $|C|$.

\subsection{Setup}
For matched edit sets we run ROME and AlphaEdit on the same Llama-3.2-1B,
CounterFact, $N{=}200$ edits $\times$ $M{=}500$ probes, under the identical
known-fact and successful-edit filters used for the gate (Section~\ref{sec:gate}).
We bin the shared (edit, probe) pairs into key-cosine quartiles and report
the \emph{absolute} mean logit-damage removed $=\text{mean}(\text{ROME})-
\text{mean}(\text{AlphaEdit})$, not a ``protection ratio'' (which is
non-monotone, an artifact of ROME's own cosine-scaling in the denominator).

\subsection{Result: monotone removal at every layer, 3 seeds}
AlphaEdit floors collateral damage to a small residual while ROME damage
scales with cosine, so the damage removed is monotone in key-cosine at
\emph{every} layer (Table~\ref{tab:causal}, Figure~\ref{fig:causal}).
\emph{The sharp test passes} at the norm-growth-dominant L14: even where
norm-growth has overtaken key-cosine as the within-probe predictor
(Section~\ref{sec:regime}), AlphaEdit still removes damage monotonically in
key-cosine (quartile removed \cbcQuartLfourteen{}) -- geometry predicts
\emph{causally removable} damage even at the one layer studied where the
correlational $\rho$ weakens.

\subsection{Projector circularity retired; holdout numbers are primary}
A referee's objection: the by-construction projector is fit on the very
probes whose damage we measure, so the tracking could be by construction. We
refit the projector on \emph{held-out} keys. The within-probe
$\rho$(key-cos, damage-removed) is unchanged, 3-seed: L8 holdout
\hoRhoLeight{}, L12 holdout \hoRhoLtwelve{}, matching the by-construction
arm (\cbcRhoLeight{}\,/\,\cbcRhoLtwelve{}); mean damage
ROME$\rightarrow$Alpha is \hoDmgLeightRome{}$\rightarrow$\hoDmgLeightAlpha{}
(L8) and \hoDmgLtwelveRome{}$\rightarrow$\hoDmgLtwelveAlpha{} (L12); removed
top-vs-bottom quartile ratio is \hoRatioLeight{}\,/\,\hoRatioLtwelve{}. These
holdout numbers are the primary circularity control reported in this paper.
A generic-key-bank projector reproduces the result directionally at the
gate level but has no aggregated damage-removed Spearman of its own, so we
note it only as a secondary, qualitative check.

\subsection{AlphaEdit erases the Qwen inversion}
On Qwen-1.5B, where ROME's coupling is negative (\qwenFifteenSigned{}),
AlphaEdit removes the geometric coupling \emph{whatever its sign}; the
deletion-setting replica shows the same collapse (Section~\ref{sec:deletion}).
The causal statement is therefore ``null-space projection removes the
geometry-carried damage regardless of the sign of the correlational law,''
stronger than a Llama-only positive result and consistent with the
architecture-conditioned account of Section~\ref{sec:dissociation}.

We rerun the causal cell -- using the held-out-key projector throughout -- on
four further settings: an instruction-tuned twin of the 1B model, a second
base architecture (GPT-J-6B), a much larger model (GPT-NeoX-20B), and the 8B
model at which the observational coupling is already known to attenuate
(Section~\ref{sec:regime}). Three of these four cells rest on three AlphaEdit
projector seeds (GPT-NeoX-20B remains single-seed); the largest seed-to-seed
spread is 0.062 in $\rho$ (8B L16) and no cell changes sign across seeds.
Table~\ref{tab:causal-scale} summarizes.

\paragraph{Instruction tuning.} On the instruction-tuned Llama-3.2-1B twin at
its canonical layer, null-space projection reproduces the 1B story at full
magnitude: mean logit-damage \instructDmgRome{} under ROME collapses to
\instructDmgAlpha{} under AlphaEdit, the signed within-probe correlation is
\instructCausalRho{}, and damage-removed rises monotonically across key-cosine
quartiles (\instructQuartLo{}$\rightarrow$\instructQuartHi{}, top-vs-bottom
\instructRatio{}$\times$). The causal law is not an artifact of the base
checkpoint; it survives instruction tuning.

\paragraph{A second base architecture (GPT-J-6B).} At the layer where we edit
GPT-J the collateral is small and sits in the net-improvement regime (mean
logit-damage \gptjDmgRome{} under ROME, \gptjDmgAlpha{} under AlphaEdit), as
it does for Qwen. The signed within-probe correlation is therefore negative
(\gptjCausalRho{}), but the \emph{magnitude} of the removed perturbation still
rises monotonically with key-cosine (absolute quartile means
\gptjQuartLo{} / $-0.021$ / $-0.035$ / \gptjQuartHi{}; we report the absolute
quartiles rather than a ratio here, because the first-quartile base
($\approx$0.01 logits) is two orders of magnitude below the 1B cell's damage
scale and any ratio built on it is arbitrarily inflatable).
This is the sign-agnostic causal statement made above for Qwen, now on a
second base architecture: projection removes a key-geometry-scaled
perturbation whatever the sign of the local damage regime.

\paragraph{Scale (GPT-NeoX-20B).} At 20B and L16, damage-removed is again
monotone across quartiles (\neoxQuartLo{}$\rightarrow$\neoxQuartHi{},
top-vs-bottom \neoxRatio{}$\times$), so the magnitude-scaling of what
projection removes holds at the largest scale we test, even though the signed
within-probe correlation here is weak (\neoxCausalRho{}).

\paragraph{8B: attenuation, not confirmation.} At 8B the causal coupling is
weak and its sign is not stable across depth: the held-out-projector
within-probe correlation is \eightbCausalRhoLsixteen{} at L16 and
\eightbCausalRhoLtwentyfour{} at L24, tracking the attenuation and
sign-dependence of the \emph{observational} coupling already documented at
this scale (Section~\ref{sec:regime}). We therefore do not read the 8B cell as
a causal confirmation. The large, positive-signed, monotone-removal form of
the law is a small-to-mid-scale result: it is
clean at 1B in both the base and instruction-tuned checkpoints; across 6B--20B
the intervention keeps removing a key-geometry-scaled perturbation but in
weaker or sign-shifted regimes; and at 8B the signed causal coupling is too
weak and inconsistent to claim. We do not report an 8B quartile ratio, which
is numerically unstable near the sign change.

\subsection{Honest caveats}
AlphaEdit's floor is not perfectly cosine-flat, so ``removes exactly
$S\cdot\|k_p\|\cdot|C|$'' is approximate, not exact. The holdout and
by-construction arms are both 3-seed and agree (L8
\hoRhoLeight{}\,/\,\cbcRhoLeight{}; L12 \hoRhoLtwelve{}\,/\,\cbcRhoLtwelve{});
the floor rises modestly under the holdout projector (mean damage
\hoDmgLeightAlpha{}\,/\,\hoDmgLtwelveAlpha{} versus $\sim$0.08
by-construction), still roughly 30$\times$ below ROME. Finally, the cells of
Table~\ref{tab:causal-scale} are three-seed generality evidence except
GPT-NeoX-20B (single seed), with the 8B cell an attenuation boundary.
