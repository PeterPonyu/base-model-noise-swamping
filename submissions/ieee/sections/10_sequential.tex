\section{Sequential No-Restore Stress (Descriptive Only)}
\label{sec:sequential}

We run four streams of 50 edits with no weight restore between them
(Llama-3.2-1B L12, recheck every 10). \emph{This section is descriptive: no
geometry-attribution claim is admissible} (Figure~\ref{fig:sequential}).

\input{figures-tex/figF_sequential}

\paragraph{Survival collapses} after 50 edits --- per-stream
\seqSurvA{}~/~\seqSurvB{}~/~\seqSurvC{}~/~\seqSurvD{}\% (pooled
\seqSurvPooled{}\%; non-monotone, so we report the range, not a point). The last
two streams' higher survival is not an install artifact: their edit-success is
\emph{higher} (\seqEsrC{}~/~\seqEsrD{}) than the first two
(\seqEsrA{}~/~\seqEsrB{}).

\paragraph{Position fragility.} $\rho$(stream-position, survival) per-stream
\seqPosA{}~/~\seqPosB{}~/~\seqPosC{}~/~\seqPosD{} (roughly 0.20--0.51), pooled
\seqPosPooled{} ($p{=}\seqPosPooledP{}$; 2-stream subset \seqPosTwoStream{},
$p{=}\seqPosTwoStreamP{}$) --- later-applied edits survive modestly more often.
The pooled value mixes heterogeneous base rates, so we lean on the per-stream
range.

\paragraph{Geometry attribution is null.} The pre-registered gate
(position-partialled forward \SxC{} exposure $\rho>0$, perm-$p<0.05$) fails:
pooled partial $\rho$ \seqHone{} ($p{=}\seqHoneP{}$); per-stream partials
$-0.01$ / $+0.08$ / $+0.03$ / $+0.16$. The raw pooled $\rho$ collapses under
position partialling, so no geometry-predictability claim for the sequential
setting enters this paper.

\paragraph{Flank layers (descriptive, 2-stream).} Survival collapse appears at
every layer, \emph{hardest at L14} (\seqLfourteenSurv{}\%): pooled
\seqLeightSurv{}\% at L8 (per-stream 28/28), \seqLfourteenSurv{}\% at L14
(per-stream 8/2), versus \seqSurvPooled{}\% at L12. Position fragility is
\emph{non-monotone} across layers ($\rho$(position, survival) \seqLeightPos{} at
L8, \seqPosPooled{} at L12, \seqLfourteenPos{} at L14 --- mildest at L14). These
2-stream flank cells do not touch the geometry-attribution verdict.
