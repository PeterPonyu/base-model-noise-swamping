\caption{The geometry law across depth.
\textbf{(a)} Layer profile of $\rho_{SC}$ (corrected $S{\times}C$, Eq.~2
normgrowth variant) and raw key-cosine $\rho_C$: both trace an inverted-U
peaking at L12 ($\rho_{SC}=0.677$, $\rho_C=0.602$) with the
$S{\times}C$ statistic strictly above the raw key-cosine at all four
tested layers.
\textbf{(b)} Bar comparison of $\rho_C$ vs.\ $\rho_{SC}$ per layer, showing
that $S{\times}C$ beats raw key-cosine at every depth
($+0.007$ / $+0.019$ / $+0.075$ / $+0.203$ margin at L8--L14).
\textbf{(c)} Within-probe gate metric (solid) vs.\ flat Spearman (dashed
grey): the flat statistic's probe-marginal leakage distorts in both
directions --- it sits above the within-probe value at L8
($0.401$ vs.\ $0.395$) and below it at L10--L14
($0.491/0.511/0.282$ vs.\ $0.534/0.602/0.301$); only the partialled
within-probe profile recovers the true L12-peaked depth law.
\textbf{(d)} Edit-level permutation-null $z$-scores: the observed within-probe
mean lies ${\approx}6$--$16$ s.d.\ above the null at all layers; the
edit-level permutation-$p$ is $\leq 0.001$ for every seed (dots =
individual seeds, bar = 3-seed mean, error bar = sample s.d., $n{-}1$).
Error bars in panels (a)--(b) are 3-seed population s.d.\ from the
artifacts; panel (c) error bars are 3-seed sample s.d.\ ($n{-}1$).}
\label{fig:geometry_law}
