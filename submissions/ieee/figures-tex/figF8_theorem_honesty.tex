\caption{Theorem and surrogate honesty.
\textbf{(a)} Proposition~1 Frobenius-identity check (fp64 brute-force vs.\
algebraic shortcut): all 12 cells (4 layers $\times$ 3 seeds) pass the
$\text{max\_bound\_ratio} \leq 1$ gate, confirming the rank-one identity holds
to fp32 precision.
\textbf{(b)} True first-order influence (direct form, Eq.~3) predicts damage
in a depth-rising pattern ($\rho\approx0.21$ at L8, rising to $0.49$ at L12),
remaining below the $S{\times}C$ reference at every layer; the factorized
form tracks qualitatively but is weaker, consistent with assumption A4$'$
not being fully met.
\textbf{(c)} A4$'$ test: $\alpha(e,p)$ sign-consistency rises from $0.67$
(L8) to $0.80$ (L12) but never reaches 1.0, and the median coefficient of
variation ($1.0$--$2.3$) indicates substantial within-probe $\alpha$
variation; A4$'$ is a plausibility condition, not a consequence of A1--A3.
\textbf{(d)} Rank agreement between true-influence ordering and $S{\times}C$
ordering is near zero at all layers ($\rho\approx0$, L12 mean
$+0.075$); $S{\times}C$ is \emph{not} a faithful rank surrogate for direct
influence, validating the Proposition~2 remark.
Error bars are 3-seed sample s.d.\ ($n{-}1$).}
