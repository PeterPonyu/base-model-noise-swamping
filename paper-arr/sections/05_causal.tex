\section{Causal Test: AlphaEdit Removes the Geometry-Predicted Damage}
\label{sec:causal}

The within-probe correlation (\S\ref{sec:regime}) and the \SxC{} algebra
(\S\ref{sec:method}) are, on their own, observational. We close the gap with an
intervention. AlphaEdit \citep{fang2025alphaedit} applies the same rank-one
target as ROME but first projects the update onto the null-space of a set of
preserved-knowledge keys, so for a preserved probe $k_p$ the induced
perturbation $\dW_\alpha\cdot k_p\approx 0$. Eq.~\ref{eq:sc} then predicts:
since ROME's collateral term is $S\cdot\|k_p\|\cdot|C|$ and AlphaEdit drives it
toward zero, the \emph{damage AlphaEdit removes} should itself rise
monotonically with $|C|$.

\paragraph{Setup.} For matched edit sets we run ROME and AlphaEdit on the same
Llama-3.2-1B, CounterFact, $N{=}200$ edits $\times$ $M{=}500$ probes, under the
identical known-fact and successful-edit filters used for the gate. We
bin the shared (edit, probe) pairs into key-cosine quartiles and report the
\emph{absolute} mean logit-damage removed $=\text{mean}(\text{ROME})-
\text{mean}(\text{AlphaEdit})$, not a ``protection ratio'' (non-monotone, an
artifact of ROME's own cosine-scaling).

\paragraph{Result (3 seeds, all four layers).} AlphaEdit floors collateral
damage to a small residual while ROME damage scales with cosine, so the damage
removed is monotone in key-cosine at \emph{every} layer, including the
norm-growth-dominant L14 (Table~\ref{tab:causal}, Figure~\ref{fig:causal}).
\textbf{The sharp test passes:} even at L14, where norm-growth has overtaken
key-cosine as the within-probe predictor, AlphaEdit still removes damage
monotonically in key-cosine (quartile removed \cbcQuartLfourteen{}) --
geometry predicts \emph{causally removable} damage even where the correlational
$\rho$ weakens.

\paragraph{Projector circularity retired; holdout numbers are primary
(3-seed).} A referee's objection: the by-construction projector is fit on the
very probes whose damage we measure, so the tracking could be by construction.
We refit the projector on \textbf{held-out} keys. The within-probe
$\rho$(key-cos, damage-removed) is unchanged, 3-seed: L8 holdout \hoRhoLeight{},
L12 holdout \hoRhoLtwelve{}, matching by-construction
(\cbcRhoLeight{}\,/\,\cbcRhoLtwelve{}); mean damage ROME$\rightarrow$Alpha
\hoDmgLeightRome{}$\rightarrow$\hoDmgLeightAlpha{} (L8) and
\hoDmgLtwelveRome{}$\rightarrow$\hoDmgLtwelveAlpha{} (L12); removed
top-vs-bottom quartile ratio \hoRatioLeight{}\,/\,\hoRatioLtwelve{}. These
holdout numbers are the primary circularity control; a generic-key-bank
projector reproduces the result directionally at gate level but has no
aggregated damage-removed Spearman, so it is not quoted in the main text.

\paragraph{AlphaEdit erases the Qwen inversion.} On Qwen-1.5B, where ROME's
coupling is negative (\qwenFifteenSigned{}), AlphaEdit removes the geometric
coupling \emph{whatever its sign}; the deletion-setting replica shows the same
collapse (\S\ref{sec:deletion}). The causal statement is therefore
``null-space projection removes the geometry-carried damage regardless of the
sign of the correlational law,'' which is stronger than a Llama-only positive
result.

\paragraph{Honest caveats.} AlphaEdit's floor is not perfectly cosine-flat, so
``removes exactly $S\cdot\|k_p\|\cdot|C|$'' is approximate, not exact. The
holdout and by-construction arms are both 3-seed and agree
(L8 \hoRhoLeight{}\,/\,\cbcRhoLeight{}; L12 \hoRhoLtwelve{}\,/\,\cbcRhoLtwelve{});
the floor rises modestly under the holdout projector (mean damage
\hoDmgLeightAlpha{}\,/\,\hoDmgLtwelveAlpha{} vs.\ $\sim$0.08 by-construction),
still $\sim$30$\times$ below ROME.
