\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Scale.} All experiments run on a single 24\,GB GPU, capping model
scale at $\le$8B parameters. The scale/regime law is verified at 1B/3B/8B, but
the coupling magnitude is \emph{attenuated at 8B} ($|\rho|$
\regimeMagAttenLo{}--\regimeMagAttenHi{} vs.\ $0.38$ at 3B); we claim a
sign-tracks-regime law, not a scale-invariant magnitude.

\paragraph{Architecture scope of the signed law.} The signed
key-cosine$\rightarrow$damage law is Llama-family-specific. Off Llama it goes
null (gemma \gemmaSigned{}, Phi \phiSigned{}) or inverts (Qwen-1.5B
\qwenFifteenSigned{}, Qwen-3B \qwenThreeSigned{}). We report these as an honest
dissociation arm; the magnitude law transfers on four of five families, with
gemma double-dead the exception.

\paragraph{Mechanism scope.} Eq.~\ref{eq:sc} is a single-layer rank-one ROME
identity. It does not describe full-rank fine-tuning (coupling $\sim$0) or
MEMIT's multi-layer spread (coupling DEAD at every layer); we report both as
null arms rather than force-fitting the algebra.

\paragraph{Remaining low-seed cells.} Single-seed cells, marked as such
in-text: MEMIT L10/L14 (\memitRhoLten{}/\memitRhoLfourteen{}); the KL-ladder at
L12 (\klLtwelveZeroThree{}/\klLtwelveOne{}/\klLtwelveThree{}/\klLtwelveFull{});
Llama-3B L14 (\llamaThreeBLfourteen{}); the zsRE-deletion transfer; and the
generic-projector causal variant. The canonical EGL table (App.~\ref{app:egl})
is 2-seed and the sequential flank layers L8/L14 (\S\ref{sec:sequential}) are
2-stream; all other headline cells are 3-seed.

\paragraph{Anisotropy is descriptive.} The anisotropy characterization
(App.~\ref{app:aniso}) is key-space-only, with no damage join and no causal
claim. The Llama-vs-Qwen crowding contrast replicates across 3 seeds, but the
per-seed spectra remain a description of the edit-key distribution, not evidence
that anisotropy causes the regime.

\paragraph{Sequential geometry attribution is unsettled.} The sequential
section is descriptive; the pre-registered geometry-attribution gate is not
passed (pooled partial \seqHone{}, $p{=}\seqHoneP{}$), and the survival result
is ordering-dependent (10--42\% range).

\paragraph{GPT-2-XL sanity.} Both GPT-2-XL sanity cells have edit-success
\gptEsrL{}/\gptEsrH{}, below our 0.9 screening threshold; we treat these as a
sanity check, not a headline.
