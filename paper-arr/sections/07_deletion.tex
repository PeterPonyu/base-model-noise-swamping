\section{Deletion Collateral: Geometry Governs a New Edit Type}
\label{sec:deletion}

The mechanism was derived and tested for knowledge \emph{rewrites}. We show it
also governs \emph{deletion} edits --- removing a learned refusal
(``I can't help with that'') --- a qualitatively different edit direction that
was not in the original design. Using the same within-probe statistic on
Llama-3.2-1B L12 (Figure~\ref{fig:deletion}):

\paragraph{Refusal-deletion collateral is geometry-governed}, and \emph{more}
strongly than rewrites: within-probe (non-double-centered) $\rho$
\delRefNonDCa{} / \delRefNonDCb{} / \delRefNonDCc{} (3 seeds; double-centered
$\rho$ \delRefDCa{} / \delRefDCb{} / \delRefDCc{}), above the rewrite reference
\rewriteRef{}. Layer profile (raw key-cosine within-probe, 3-seed): L8

\delRefLfourteen{}\,$\pm$\,\delRefLfourteenSD{} (with the L12 raw aggregate

\paragraph{Variant robustness (two distinct statistics, do not conflate).} Two
statistics exist per variant and must be reported separately: (a) the
\emph{gate \SxC{} statistic} (seed-0): non-double-centered \SxC{}-vs-damage
Spearman and its double-centered value; (b) the \emph{raw key-cosine
within-probe statistic} (3-seed).
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{eos} is robust on \emph{both}: gate non-DC \SxC{} \eosGateSxC{}
  (seed-0), and raw key-cosine within-probe \eosRaw{}\,$\pm$\,\eosRawSD{} (3-seed).
\item \textbf{suppress} is the \emph{fragile} variant: its gate non-DC \SxC{}
  is \suppressGateSxC{} (seed-0) but double-centering-fragile
  ($\rightarrow\sim$\suppressGateDC{}); and on the raw key-cosine within-probe
  metric it is \suppressRaw{}\,$\pm$\,\suppressRawSD{} (3-seed, DEAD). These are
  \emph{different} statistics, not a discrepancy: the weak raw coupling is
  consistent with the double-centering fragility, so we report suppress as the
  fragile variant, never swapping the two numbers.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Causal (3-seed).} AlphaEdit-delete collapses damage
\alphaDelDmgPre{}$\rightarrow\sim$\alphaDelDmgPost{} \emph{and} the coupling to
\alphaDelRho{}\,$\pm$\,\alphaDelRhoSD{} (3-seed, DEAD) --- the same ``removes
the geometry-carried damage whatever its sign'' result as the rewrite arm.

\paragraph{Llama-scoping replicates in deletion (3-seed).} Qwen-1.5B-delete
$\rho$ \qwenDelRho{}\,$\pm$\,\qwenDelRhoSD{} at mean damage \qwenDelDmg{}
(improvement regime) --- the same off-Llama behavior as the rewrite arm.

\paragraph{Transplant gate.} The \SxC{} surrogate beats a learned transplant
baseline: $\Delta\rho$ (\SxC{} minus \emph{best} transplant)
\transDeltaLtwelveA{} / \transDeltaLtwelveB{} / \transDeltaLtwelveC{} at L12
(seeds), \transDeltaLeight{} at L8, \transDeltaLfourteen{} at L14.

\paragraph{Deletion transfers to zsRE (dataset generality; single-seed,
refusal, L10).} Refusal-deletion on zsRE \citep{levy2017zsre} at L10: raw key-cosine within-probe
\zsreDelRaw{} (PASS, survives the permutation battery); formal gate \SxC{}
non-DC \zsreDelNonDC{} $\rightarrow$ DC \zsreDelDC{} (\emph{double-centering-robust},
the opposite of suppress's fragility). This sits below the zsRE rewrite law
(\zsreRewrite{}, same layer) and CounterFact deletion (\delRefNonDCa{}, L12), so
geometry governs deletion collateral across datasets, attenuating with the
weaker zsRE signal.
