\section{Depth and Regime Transition}
\label{sec:regime}

\paragraph{The within-probe layer law (C2).} Fixing a probe, damage ranks
across edits by $S\cdot|C|$; when $S$ has limited spread relative to $C$, the
within-probe Spearman down the column is high. Across 3 seeds on the saved
gate matrices, the clean key-cosine metric traces an inverted-U in depth:
$\rho = \gWithinLeight{}\rightarrow\gWithinLten{}\rightarrow
\gWithinLtwelve{}$ (peak, L12) $\rightarrow\gWithinLfourteen{}$ (L14), with
$\text{frac\_positive}=1.0$ and permutation-$p$ at the \gPermFloor{} floor at
every layer (Figure~\ref{fig:layerlaw}).

\paragraph{The L14 crossover.} The rank of damage down a column is governed by
the relative spread of $S$ vs.\ $|C|$. Mid-layer, $|C|$ carries most
cross-edit variance and geometry dominates; at L14, $|C|$ saturates (mean
$|C|$ rises to \meanCLfourteen{} vs.\ \meanCbandLo{}--\meanCbandHi{} across the
L8--L12 band) while $S$ becomes the dominant varying factor, so
matrix-norm-growth overtakes. Head-to-head, the true norm-growth$\rightarrow$damage
within-probe $\rho$ is \ngWithinLeight{} / \ngWithinLten{} / \ngWithinLtwelve{}
/ \ngWithinLfourteen{}: key-cosine wins at L8/L10/L12, and \textbf{norm-growth
overtakes at L14} (\ngWithinLfourteen{} $>$ \gWithinLfourteen{}), on all 3
seeds. This crossover is a genuine effect on the confound-clean metric, not a
flat-statistics artifact.

\paragraph{The scale/regime law.} The transition is not merely a depth effect
but a \emph{damage-regime} effect: the \textbf{sign} of the coupling tracks the
\textbf{sign of the mean damage}. At Llama-3B L24 (positive-damage regime) the
within-probe $\rho = \regimeThreeB{}$ (3-seed \regimeThreeBLo{}--\regimeThreeBHi{});
at Llama-8B L24 (net-improvement regime, mean per-edit damage negative)
$\rho = \regimeEightB{}$ (3-seed mean, per-seed \regimeEightBHi{} to
\regimeEightBLo{}). Within 8B the sign flips with depth: L24 \regimeEightB{}
(3-seed), L16 $+\regimeEightBLsixteen{}$ / L28 $+\regimeEightBLtwentyeight{}$
(seed-0). The norm-growth transition tracks \emph{relative} depth: 8B L28 sits
at \relDepthEightBLtwentyeight{} relative depth (the 1B-L14 position) and shows
the norm-growth dominance directionally, attenuated $\sim\ngAttenPct\%$ vs.\ 1B.
The settled claim is: \textbf{the sign tracks the damage regime (3-seed both
sides); the coupling magnitude is attenuated at 8B}
($|\rho|\,\regimeMagAttenLo{}$--$\regimeMagAttenHi{}$ vs.\ $0.38$ at 3B) -- it is
not a scale-invariant magnitude.
